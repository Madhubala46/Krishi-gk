Our Services

One of the keys to our survival is how

Krishi GK is a portal designed to provide farmers with timely and relevant information. To help farmers make better plans, we offer simple updates on weather forecasts, government programs, mandi prices, and farming news. To help increase output while conserving resources, we also offer soil testing, crop health advice, pest management advice, and intelligent irrigation recommendations. Farmers can also interact with specialists, learn about sustainable and organic farming methods, and get information that helps them with day-to-day farming. Krishi GK aims to increase farming s sustainability, profitability, and productivity for all growers.

Krishi GK is available to help you every step of the way, whether your goal is improved crop management, market knowledge, or risk mitigation techniques.
